Who I am

Muhammad Hasnain

I'm a Full Stack Developer with a passion for building premium, high-performance web applications. My work sits at the intersection of clean engineering and thoughtful design, with a growing focus on practical AI integration.

I care about the details most people skip: load times, spacing, copy, the way a button feels when you press it. Every project I take on gets that same level of attention, whether it's a five-page brochure site or a full AI-powered platform. I'm also the co-founder of Fyrelanes, a full-service digital agency where I apply the same standards across web development, app development, and design to help brands ship complete digital products, not just websites.
